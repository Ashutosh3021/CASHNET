---
name: CASHNET decisions
description: Durable product boundaries for the CASHNET investigative intelligence platform.
---

CASHNET is synthetic-first and case-centric: the same linked records must drive report ingestion, graph/timeline, crypto conversion, risk, geospatial prediction, intervention, audit, and reporting.

**Why:** The product specification requires a complete workflow without implying access to confidential government, banking, VASP, or blockchain systems.

**How to apply:** Preserve provenance labels, expose exact conversion timestamps, keep unknown entities unknown, and require explicit investigator approval before any intervention submission.
