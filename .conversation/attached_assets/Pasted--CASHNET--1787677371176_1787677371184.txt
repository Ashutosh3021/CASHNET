1. PROJECT CONTEXT
============================================================

Build a complete full-stack investigative intelligence platform
named:

CASHNET

Full name:

Cybercrime Financial Flow, Blockchain Intelligence &
Predictive Cash-Out Analysis Network

Target stakeholder / intended institutional context:

Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)
CIS Division
Ministry of Home Affairs
Government of India

CASHNET is a research/prototype decision-support platform intended
to assist authorized cybercrime investigators in analyzing
financial and cryptocurrency fund movement associated with
cybercrime complaints.

The system must work completely using SYNTHETIC DATA for the MVP.

It must be architected so that authorized real-world data
providers, banking systems, blockchain intelligence providers,
government systems, and VASP systems can be integrated later
through secure APIs.

DO NOT claim that CASHNET currently has direct access to:

- NCRP
- SAHYOG
- bank internal systems
- UPI internal systems
- law-enforcement databases
- VASP internal databases
- confidential government systems

unless explicitly configured with authorized APIs.

Use synthetic data and clearly label it as synthetic.

3. IMPORTANT ADVERSARIAL ASSUMPTIONS
============================================================

Do NOT assume that fraudsters:

- use the same bank as the victim
- use one bank account
- use one mule account
- use one wallet
- directly withdraw funds
- stay within India
- use only one blockchain
- use only one VASP
- transfer funds linearly
- immediately cash out
- keep the same identity across accounts
- use obvious transaction patterns

The system must support complex fund movement.

Example:

Victim
  ↓
Bank Account A
  ↓
Mule Account B
  ↓
Mule Account C
  ├────────→ Account D
  ├────────→ VASP A
  └────────→ Wallet A

Wallet A
  ↓
Wallet B
  ↓
Foreign VASP
  ↓
Foreign Wallet
  ↓
Bank Account
  ↓
ATM / Cash-out
